WabuGBA Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

WabuGBA is a retro handheld emulator application for Android. This privacy policy explains how the application handles user data.

Information Collection

WabuGBA does not collect personal user information directly.

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising purposes. These services may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, diagnostic information, and app interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Local Files

WabuGBA allows users to load locally stored ROM files and save data. These files remain on the user's device and are not uploaded to external servers by WabuGBA.

Advertising

WabuGBA may display non-intrusive advertisements using Google AdMob. Google may use data to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising experiences.

Data Security

Data transmitted by third-party services is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS security.

Children

WabuGBA is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated in the future as the application evolves.
